Meldra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Meldra (com.meldra.app) is provided by Fady Fayez Younan. Questions about this policy can be sent to fadyfayez067@gmail.com.

Gameplay and information on your device

Meldra does not require an account. Solo gameplay runs on your device. Local multiplayer connects devices over the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot; it does not use a Meldra internet game server.

The app stores your chosen player name, avatar, player identifier, app preferences, room defaults, and recent room connection information on your device. Recent connection information includes the room code, host name, local network address, port, reconnect capability and time last seen. It supports reconnecting to a recent room. A live match is held in the host phone's memory.

Android may include local app data in device backup or device-transfer processes, depending on device settings. Such services are governed by their providers.

Information exchanged with nearby players

When you host or join a room, Meldra exchanges player names, selected avatars, player identifiers and game actions with participating devices. Room discovery advertises room information on the local network. The host processes the game state and sends each player their own hand and the shared game information.

Local gameplay uses unencrypted local network connections. Use a network and players you trust. Keep room QR codes and reconnect information private.

Camera and QR scanning

Camera access is used for scanning room QR codes. You can decline camera access and enter a room code instead. Meldra does not save camera photos or upload camera frames to a Meldra server.

On Android, the QR scanner uses Google's ML Kit through the mobile_scanner library. Google states that image processing happens on the device and that input images and detection results are not sent to its servers. ML Kit can send diagnostic and usage information to Google, including device and app details, per-installation identifiers, performance data and scanning event information. It may also contact Google for SDK maintenance information. See ML Kit privacy information, ML Kit Android data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy.

Other permissions and services

Network and Wi-Fi permissions support local discovery and multiplayer. Vibration supports optional haptic feedback. The app can keep the screen awake while playing. This release does not include advertising or in-app purchases, and does not include a Meldra analytics or remote crash-reporting service.

The store used to download the app and the device operating system have their own data practices, which are not controlled by Meldra.

Your choices and retention

You can change your player name, avatar and preferences in the app, revoke camera permission in Android settings, or leave a room to end participation. Local preferences remain until changed or cleared. Saved room information supports short-term reconnection; stale information is not used to rejoin an expired room.

Android's app storage controls let you clear local app data. This resets your profile and preferences. Backup providers may retain separate copies under their own policies. We do not hold a Meldra server copy of your local game data.
